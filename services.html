An AI agent that performs a technical audit of your website's legal and regulatory document readiness.

What It Checks

Privacy Policy document structure, cookie usage and consent flow, newsletter GDPR consent, AI Act Art. 50 transparency disclosure, Terms of Service, Disclaimer (business ID, seat), contact information completeness, TDM reservation in robots.txt.

What You Get

A technical checklist report — green items (OK), yellow items (needs attention), red items (missing). Each item includes a concrete recommendation on what to add or change.
