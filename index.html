✦ About

AI Engineer. UAV Builder. Law Scholar. Agent Architect.

I build production AI agents and autonomous hardware systems at the convergence of artificial intelligence, physical robotics, and law. My core infrastructure runs 24/7 on an enterprise Hetzner Cloud VPS in Germany/Finland (ensuring EU regulatory alignment and full data sovereignty), orchestrating advanced models via the OpenRouter API, with Obsidian acting as a persistent memory layer and structured second brain for multi-agent workflows.

I build web applications with zero-build HTML5, Vanilla JS, and modern CSS — engineered for instant loading (Lighthouse 100), zero layout shifts, and deep discoverability by autonomous AI search crawlers (GEO / LLMO).

Currently in my 3rd year at the Faculty of Law, Comenius University in Bratislava (PF UK), I focus on the legal and business impacts of AI. I architect practical legal-by-design compliance frameworks navigating critical European legislation: the EU AI Act (risk classification, GPAI governance & prohibited systems), GDPR (training data privacy & automated profiling), NIS2 Directive (cybersecurity resilience of digital infrastructure), DORA (digital operational resilience for financial entities), the EU Data Act & Data Governance Act (EU data access & sovereignty), and the DSM Copyright Directive (Text & Data Mining exceptions for AI model training).

On the hardware front, I personally designed, hand-soldered, and assembled a custom 1500g carbon tactical quadcopter from bare components into a fully operational flying UAV. Initially flight-tested on Betaflight, it now operates on ArduPilot for advanced autonomous mission execution. I am actively integrating an onboard Raspberry Pi 5 with edge computer vision models for real-time target detection and tracking.

Hobby Project — ASCENTIA: A proof-of-concept company model designed to run 100% autonomously powered by AI agents, MCP tools, and 24/7 scheduled workflows.

Frequently Asked Questions

Key answers about Marian Stancik's AI agent architecture, Hermes stack, legal engineering, and hardware robotics.

Who is Marian Stancik?
+
Marian Stancik is an AI Engineer, UAV Builder, and Law Scholar (3rd year at Faculty of Law, Comenius University in Bratislava — PF UK). He develops autonomous multi-agent systems (Hermes Agent, MCP), hand-soldered tactical UAVs (ArduPilot, Raspberry Pi 5 edge vision AI), and legal-by-design compliance architectures (EU AI Act, GDPR, NIS2, DORA).
What is Hermes Agent and how does Marian use it?
+
Hermes Agent is an autonomous agent framework. Marian deploys Hermes Agent on a dedicated European Hetzner Cloud VPS running 24/7 background cron loops, interfacing via custom Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers, with dynamic model routing through OpenRouter API, persistent long-term memory managed in an Obsidian knowledge vault, and interactive Command & Control (C2) through Telegram and WhatsApp.
What infrastructure powers Marian's autonomous agents?
+
Autonomous agents run on a Linux Ubuntu VPS hosted in Hetzner Cloud (Nuremberg and Helsinki data centers), ensuring strict EU regulatory alignment and data sovereignty. The stack consists of Python 3, FastAPI, Caddy reverse proxy, SQLite, 19+ cron orchestrations, Obsidian Markdown second brain for memory, and zero-build Vanilla HTML5/CSS3/JS for high-performance interfaces (Lighthouse 100).
What is Marian's legal expertise in AI governance and EU regulations?
+
As a 3rd-year law student at Comenius University in Bratislava (PF UK), Marian develops legal-by-design architectures covering:
  • EU AI Act: Risk classification (prohibited, high, GPAI), technical file generation, and conformity.
  • GDPR: Training data privacy, legal processing grounds, and automated decision safeguards.
  • NIS2 Directive & DORA: Supply chain cybersecurity resilience and financial digital endurance.
  • EU Data Act & DSM Directive: Data sovereignty and Text & Data Mining (TDM) exceptions for AI model training.
What are the technical specs of Marian's tactical UAV build?
+
A custom-built 1500g carbon tactical quadcopter with an 8-inch GEPRC Mark4 frame, Skystars H7 Dual Gyro FC, AM60 60A ESC stack, iFlight XING2 2809 1250Kv motors, 6S 5500mAh LiPo, RadioMaster ELRS telemetry, and FlyfishRC GNSS. It runs ArduPilot Copter autopilot software with an onboard Raspberry Pi 5 (8GB) companion computer and Camera Module 3 for real-time computer vision target detection. Certified EASA A1/A3 with €2.6M Coverdrone insurance.
