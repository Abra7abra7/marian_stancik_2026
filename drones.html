Built. Flown. Programmed.

1500g custom carbon quad with ArduPilot, Pixhawk 6C, Raspberry Pi 5 edge AI. EASA A1/A3 certified, €2.6M insured.

20s outdoor compilation — field flight, waypoints — no audio

Build & specifications

Frame & Motors

  • Frame: GEPRC Mark4-8 (Rotorama)
  • Motors: iFlight XING2 2809 1250Kv × 6 (Rotorama)
  • Props: HQprop LR8X4.2X3 × 8 (Rotorama)
  • Battery: GNB 5500mAh 6S 70C XT60 (Rotorama)

Electronics & Flight Controller

  • FC+ESC: Skystars H7 Dual Gyro + AM60 AM32 (Rotorama)
  • Receiver: Radiomaster XR1 Nano ELRS 868MHz/2.4GHz (Rotorama)
  • GPS+Compass: FlyfishRC M10QMC-5883L (Rotorama)
  • Capacitor: 1000μF 50V (SvetSúčiastok)

AI & Autopilot

  • AI Computer: Raspberry Pi 5 — 8GB RAM (RPishop)
  • BEC: 2-13S 5V 5A for RPi (Rotorama)
  • Camera: Raspberry Pi Camera 3 (RPishop)
  • SD Card: Raspberry Pi 64GB Class A2 (RPishop)
  • Cooler: Raspberry Pi 5 Active Cooler (RPishop)
  • Servo: Micro Servo MG90S — release mechanism (RCHobby)
  • USB-C pigtail: JOY-IT USB-C power cable 1m (Alza)

FPV System

  • FPV Camera: Caddx Ratel 2 Micro (Rotorama)
  • VTX: Axisflying Smurfs 25-1600mW (Rotorama)
  • VTX Antenna: Foxeer Lollipop 4 Plus × 2 (Rotorama)
  • Goggles: Skyzone Cobra X V4 (Rotorama)

Ground Control

  • Radio: RadioMaster Pocket ELRS 2.4GHz (Rotorama)
  • Radio Batt: Samsung 35E 18650 3500mAh × 2 (Rotorama)
  • Charger: iSDT 608AC 50/200W (Rotorama)
  • Lipo Bag: Rotorama Lipo Safe bag (Rotorama)
