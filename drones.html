Hand-Built. Flight-Tested. AI-Augmented.

Custom 1500g carbon quadcopter hand-soldered from bare components. Initially flown on Betaflight, powered by ArduPilot, with onboard Raspberry Pi 5 edge vision AI in development. EASA A1/A3 certified, €2.6M insured.

20s outdoor compilation — flight testing & autonomous waypoint navigation

Hardware Specifications & Build Components

Frame & Propulsion

  • Frame GEPRC Mark4-8 8" Carbon
  • Motors iFlight XING2 2809 1250Kv × 6
  • Propellers HQprop LR8X4.2X3 Triblade × 8
  • Main Battery GNB 5500mAh 6S 70C XT60 LiPo

Electronics & Flight Controller

  • FC & ESC Stack Skystars H7 Dual Gyro + AM60 AM32 60A
  • Autopilot Firmware ArduPilot Copter (Betaflight tested)
  • Receiver RadioMaster XR1 Nano ELRS
  • GPS & Compass FlyfishRC M10QMC-5883L GNSS
  • Power Filter 1000μF 50V Low-ESR Capacitor

Onboard Edge AI & Vision

  • AI Companion Computer Raspberry Pi 5 (8GB RAM)
  • Dedicated BEC 2-13S 5V 5A Step-Down Module
  • Vision Sensor Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3
  • Storage High-Endurance 64GB Class A2 MicroSD
  • Cooling & Servo Active Cooler + MG90S Payload Servo

FPV Video & Transmission

  • FPV Camera Caddx Ratel 2 Micro Starlight
  • Video Transmitter (VTX) Axisflying Smurfs 25-1600mW 5.8GHz
  • Antennas Foxeer Lollipop 4 Plus High-Gain × 2

Ground Control & Power Infrastructure
